# Voter Rights and Grievance Redressal

## Fundamental Voting Rights
- **Right to Vote**: Every citizen aged 18+ can vote (Article 326).
- **Right to Secrecy**: Your vote is secret. No one can compel you to reveal it.
- **Right to Vote Without Fear**: Coercion is a criminal offense (Section 171 IPC).
- **Right to Paid Leave**: Employers must give paid leave on polling day (Section 135B RPA 1951).
- **Right to Information**: Candidates must declare criminal records and assets publicly.
- **Right to Reject**: Press NOTA if you don't wish to vote for any candidate.

## Grievance Redressal

### Call ECI Helpline
- **Dial 1950** (toll-free during elections)
- Report booth capture, intimidation, bogus voting, EVM malfunction

### Online Complaint
- Visit eci.gov.in/grievances/
- Use **cVIGIL App** to report MCC violations with photo/video
- Flying squads dispatched within 100 minutes

### Forms for Grievances
- **Form 7**: Object to inclusion of a name (deceased/shifted persons)
- **Form 8**: Correction of entries (name, age, photo, address)
- **Form 8A**: Transposition within same constituency

## What To Do If Your Name Is Missing
1. Check at voterportal.eci.gov.in
2. File Form 6 immediately
3. Call 1950 and inform BLO
4. Contact District Election Officer if within 10 days of polling

## What To Do If Someone Voted In Your Name
1. Inform the Presiding Officer immediately
2. You may receive a tendered ballot
3. File an FIR at the nearest police station
4. Report on cVIGIL or call 1950
