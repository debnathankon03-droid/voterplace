# Voter Registration in India

## Who Can Register?
- Any Indian citizen who is 18 years or older on the qualifying date (January 1 of the year of electoral roll revision) can register as a voter.
- You must be a resident of the constituency where you wish to register.
- Persons of unsound mind as declared by a competent court cannot register.

## How to Register — Form 6
- **Form 6** is the application for new voter registration.
- You can fill Form 6 **online** at [nvsp.in](https://www.nvsp.in/) or through the **Voter Helpline App**.
- You can also submit a physical form at your nearest Electoral Registration Officer (ERO) office.

### Documents Required for Form 6
1. **Age proof** (any one): Birth certificate, Class 10 marksheet, Passport, Aadhaar card
2. **Address proof** (any one): Aadhaar card, Ration card, Utility bill (water/electricity), Bank passbook, Rent agreement
3. **Passport-size photograph** (recent, color)
4. **Aadhaar number** (optional but recommended for linking)

## EPIC — Electoral Photo Identity Card
- After registration is approved, you receive an **EPIC** (commonly called "Voter ID card").
- EPIC contains: Name, photo, father's/husband's name, date of birth, address, constituency, and a unique EPIC number.
- If you've lost your EPIC, you can download a digital copy called **e-EPIC** from the Voter Portal.

## Timeline for Registration
- Online applications are typically processed within **30 days**.
- A Booth Level Officer (BLO) may visit your address for verification.
- Track your application status on nvsp.in using your reference number.

## Pre-Registration at 17
- If you are 17 years old, you can **pre-register** so your name is added to the roll once you turn 18.
- Use Form 6 and mark the appropriate qualifying date.

## Changing Your Details
- **Form 8** — Correction of entries (name, photo, address within the same constituency)
- **Form 8A** — Transposition (shifting within the same constituency to a different polling area)
- **Form 6** — Fresh registration (if moving to a new constituency)
- **Form 7** — Objection to inclusion of a name (to remove deceased/shifted persons)
