# The Polling Process

## Before Polling Day
- **Check your name** on the Electoral Roll at voterportal.eci.gov.in or by calling 1950.
- **Find your polling station** using the Voter Helpline App or the ECI website.
- **Carry valid ID**: EPIC (Voter ID) is preferred. If you don't have EPIC, any of these 12 alternative IDs work: Aadhaar, Passport, Driving License, PAN Card, Service ID (for government employees), Student ID with photo, Bank passbook with photo, MNREGA Job Card, Pension document with photo, MP/MLA/MLC identity card, Smart Card issued by RGI under NPR, Health Insurance Smart Card.

## On Polling Day — Step by Step
1. **Go to your designated polling station** (you cannot vote at any other station).
2. **Queue up** — separate queues may exist for men, women, and persons with disabilities.
3. **Identity verification** — the Presiding Officer checks your name against the voter list and verifies your ID.
4. **Indelible ink** — your left index finger is marked with indelible ink to prevent repeat voting.
5. **Receive a ballot slip** — this has your serial number.
6. **Enter the voting compartment** — it ensures secrecy of your vote.
7. **Press the button** on the **EVM (Electronic Voting Machine)** next to your chosen candidate's name and symbol.
8. **Check the VVPAT slip** — the **Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail** machine displays your chosen candidate's name and symbol for 7 seconds. Verify it matches.
9. **Exit** the polling station.

## EVM — Electronic Voting Machine
- India uses EVMs since 2004 (fully since 2004 general elections).
- Each EVM can record up to 2,000 votes.
- EVMs are standalone — they are NOT connected to any network or the internet.
- They run on batteries, so power cuts do not affect voting.
- Each EVM is tested (mock poll) before actual voting begins.

## VVPAT — Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail
- Attached to every EVM since 2019 (fully deployed in the 2019 General Elections).
- After you press the EVM button, the VVPAT prints a slip showing the candidate's name, serial number, and symbol.
- The slip is visible for 7 seconds through a glass window, then drops into a sealed box.
- VVPAT slips of 5 randomly selected booths per constituency are matched against EVM counts for verification.

## NOTA — None of the Above
- If you don't wish to vote for any candidate, you can press the **NOTA** button on the EVM.
- NOTA was introduced by the Supreme Court of India in 2013.
- NOTA votes are counted but do not affect the result — the candidate with the most votes wins even if NOTA gets more.

## Polling Hours
- Typically **7:00 AM to 6:00 PM**, but this can vary by state and region.
- In certain areas (e.g., Naxal-affected regions), hours may be shortened for security.
- **Important**: If you are in the queue at the time of closing (6:00 PM), you WILL be allowed to vote.

## Persons with Disabilities
- PWD voters can take a companion of their choice into the voting compartment.
- Wheelchairs and ramps must be provided at all polling stations.
- Braille-enabled EVMs are available at select stations.
- You can request a postal ballot if you are unable to travel to the booth.

## Postal Ballots
- Available for: Service voters (military/paramilitary), government employees on election duty, persons with disability (40%+), senior citizens (80+), COVID-affected persons, essential services workers.
- Apply through Form 12 well in advance.

## Key Rules
- **No campaigning** within 100 meters of a polling station.
- **No mobile phones/cameras** inside the voting compartment.
- **Secrecy of vote**: It is a criminal offense to reveal how someone voted or to try to find out.
